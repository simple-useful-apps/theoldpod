Privacy Policy

The Old Pod · last updated July 2026

The Old Pod collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no server of its own. The developer receives no data about you or your use of the app — none is gathered, transmitted, or stored anywhere the developer can see.

Your music library is a folder of files on your own device and in your own iCloud Drive. The app reads and plays those files locally. If you use iCloud, syncing those files between your devices is handled entirely by Apple's iCloud Drive under Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to your iCloud content.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, or delete on our end, and there are no data-retention or access requests to make.
