theoldpod

A music player for people who own their music.

theoldpod is a pair of apps — iPhone and Mac — that play the MP3s you already have. There is no store, no streaming, no account to create, and no ads. Your library is a folder of files in your iCloud Drive; the apps are two views onto that folder.

Drag an album in from the Finder, and it's on your phone. Delete a file, and it's gone. The files are the truth; the app never holds your music hostage.

How it works

  • A folder of MP3s. That's the whole library. Nothing is imported into a vault, nothing is locked in.
  • iCloud Drive keeps devices in step. Your existing iCloud — no new account, no separate service. Fully offline once files are on the device, and a purely local folder works too.
  • Nothing else. No recommendations, no badges, no analytics, nothing collected. The app wants nothing from you.

What it does

  • Artists, Albums, Songs, Playlists both apps
  • Queue, shuffle, repeat, search both apps
  • Lock Screen & Control Center playback iPhone
  • Sidebar, song table, media keys, mini player Mac

It works the way music software used to, back when it did less and did it well: you see your music, you pick something, and it plays.
